## 長描述 / Full description（Play 上限 4000 字元）

### 中文（繁）

召喚機械礦工，敲爛一座又一座廢料山，睇住碎料自動上帶送入熔爐，煉成現金、零件同環保
值——呢個係一隻唔使一直盯住手機都會賺緊錢嘅放置大亨遊戲。

**核心玩法**
- 召喚最多 12 個機械礦工，自動敲山、自動送帶入爐，你唔喺線都繼續賺（離線收入最多結
  算 8 小時）。
- 升級輸送帶、礦工效率、精煉爐，數值曲線設計到有得一直谷落去。
- 每隔一段時間開一次 120 秒「狂熱車場」：親手揸主戰車衝落一堆會爆散嘅金幣同齒輪，
  穿過 ×2／×3／×4／×5 倍數門谷分，仲有刺滾筒、窄岩浆同限重木橋等風險關卡，考你手感
  同判斷。
- 賺夠威望門檻可以重置場地，換取永久收入加成，愈玩愈快。
- 場地會逐步解鎖新區域（礦坑核心 → 更多場地），跟住你嘅進度慢慢擴張。

**點解免費玩到咁盡**
- 遊戲完全免費，睇獎勵廣告可以攞額外好處（例如離線收入 ×2、免費開多次狂熱），**唔
  睇廣告一樣可以正常玩完成個遊戲**，廣告淨係加成，唔係逼你睇。
- 一次性內購可以完全移除廣告，價錢公道，冇訂閱、冇扭蛋、冇抽卡。

**冇嘅嘢（等你放心）**
- 冇強制登入、冇社交比較排行榜逼你課金、冇隨機抽獎（loot box）、冇 pay-to-win 數值
  牆——升級曲線人人一樣。

而家先第一階段上架，我哋會持續跟進玩家意見調整數值同新增內容。多謝支持！

### English

Summon robot miners, smash junk pile after junk pile, and watch the ore ride the belt
into the furnace to become cash, components, and eco points — a classic idle-tycoon loop
that keeps earning even while you're not looking.

**Core loop**
- Summon up to 12 robot miners who dig automatically and feed the furnace via conveyor
  belt — you keep earning offline (up to 8 hours of offline earnings settled per visit).
- Upgrade your belt, miner speed, and refining line on curves designed for satisfying
  long-term progression.
- Every so often, jump into a 120-second "Frenzy Yard": drive a truck through a pile of
  bursting coins and gears, pass through ×2/×3/×4/×5 multiplier gates, and dodge risk
  elements like spike rollers, a narrow lava pit, and a weight-limited bridge that reward
  good timing and judgment.
- Hit a prestige threshold to reset your field for a permanent income boost and progress
  even faster next run.
- New zones unlock progressively as you grow — the same field expands under your feet.

**Fair free-to-play**
- The game is completely free. Watching a rewarded ad grants a bonus (like 2× offline
  earnings or an extra free Frenzy run) — but **you can finish the whole game without
  watching a single ad**; ads are optional boosts, never a requirement.
- A single, fairly-priced one-time purchase removes ads entirely. No subscriptions, no
  gacha, no card packs.

**What you won't find**
- No forced login, no social leaderboard pressure to spend, no loot boxes, no pay-to-win
  wall — everyone climbs the same upgrade curve.

This is our stage-1 launch — we'll keep tuning balance and adding content based on player
feedback. Thanks for playing!

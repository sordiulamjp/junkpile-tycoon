中文版

概覽

Junkpile Tycoon（下稱「本 App」）係一隻放置／大亨風格嘅單機手機遊戲。本 App 冇帳戶登入、 冇雲端存檔、冇聊天或社交功能。本政策講清楚本 App 同佢用嘅第三方服務（Google AdMob／Google Play）會處理咩資料。

我們（開發者）直接收集嘅資料

冇。本 App 唔要求登入、唔收集姓名／電郵／電話等個人身份資料，亦冇自己嘅 後台伺服器儲存玩家資料。遊戲內建一個純本機事件記錄（存喺你部裝置度，上限 500 行自動滾動覆蓋舊記錄），淨係喺你主動匯出並傳畀我哋作支援用途時先會離開你部裝置。

第三方服務收集嘅資料

  • Google AdMob（含 Google 使用者訊息平台 UMP 同意表格）：為咗顯示獎勵廣告， Google AdMob 可能收集廣告 ID、大約位置（由 IP 推算）、裝置資訊，用嚟投放同量度廣告成效。 歐洲經濟區／英國等地區用戶會見到 UMP 同意表格；App 設定 child-directed = false。 詳見 Google 私隱政策 同 AdMob 資料揭露。
  • Google Play Billing（去廣告付費項目）：購買由 Google Play 全程處理，我哋 見唔到你嘅付款方式資料。
  • Google Play 服務（自動診斷）：Google Play Console 自動收集嘅崩潰／ANR 報告，屬於平台本身機制。
  • 遠端遊戲設定（Cloudflare Workers，未部署時停用）：一次公開 GET request 攞遊戲數值設定，唔帶任何個人資料。

本機存檔

所有遊戲進度存喺你部裝置本機檔案，冇雲端同步。解除安裝或清除 App 資料就會一併刪除。

兒童

本 App 唔係專為兒童設計，亦冇參加 Google Play「Designed for Families」計劃。

你嘅選擇同權利

  • 停用個人化廣告／重置廣告 ID：Android 設定 → Google → 廣告。
  • 刪除本機存檔：解除安裝 App 即可。
  • 其他查閱／更正／刪除請求（PDPO 或你所在地區私隱法例）：電郵聯絡我哋。

English

Overview

Junkpile Tycoon ("the App") is a single-player idle/tycoon mobile game with no account login, no cloud save, and no chat or social features. This policy explains what data the App and the third-party services it uses (Google AdMob / Google Play) process.

Data we (the developer) collect directly

None. The App does not require sign-in and does not collect personally identifying information. We do not operate a backend server that stores player data. The App keeps a local-only gameplay event log on your device (capped at 500 lines, oldest entries rolled off automatically); it only leaves your device if you choose to export and send it to us for support.

Data collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob (incl. Google User Messaging Platform / UMP consent form): to show rewarded video ads, Google AdMob may collect advertising ID, approximate (IP-derived) location, and device information. Users in the EEA/UK see a UMP consent form. The App sets child-directed = false. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure.
  • Google Play Billing (ad-removal purchase): handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • Google Play services (automatic diagnostics): crash/ANR reports collected by the platform itself.
  • Remote game-balance configuration (Cloudflare Workers, disabled until deployed): a public GET request with no personal data.

Local save data

All game progress is stored locally on your device with no cloud sync. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes it permanently.

Children

The App is not designed for children and is not enrolled in Google Play's "Designed for Families" program.

Your choices and rights

  • Opt out of personalized ads / reset advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Delete local save data: uninstall the App.
  • Other access/correction/deletion requests (PDPO or your local privacy law): email us.
